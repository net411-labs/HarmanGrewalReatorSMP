🏡 Buying in Surrey, Langley or Delta this fall? You have more leverage than you think.

01 The price. Offers below asking are back on the table.
02 Subject clauses. Keep your inspection and financing conditions.
03 Completion date. Pick timing that fits your mortgage or lease.
04 What stays. Appliances, window coverings, even furniture.
05 Repairs or credits. Turn inspection findings into a fix or a price credit.
